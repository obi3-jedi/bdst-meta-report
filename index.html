Leader + base pairings

Decks are read from the decklist name (“Leader, Subtitle - Base”). Players without a submitted list are grouped as Unknown. Common bases are interchangeable within a colour, so by default they go by colour: “Red” is any plain 30 HP Aggression base, “Red Force” any Aggression Force base (28 HP), and “Red Splash” any 27 HP Aggression base that ignores an aspect. A deck reads “Yoda Red Force”. Rare bases such as Data Vault keep their names. Top cut is read from playoff status (Active or Eliminated); without a playoff phase the top 8 by rank are used.
