Deck Breakdown

Common bases go by colour: “Yoda Red” is Yoda on any plain 30 HP Aggression base, “Yoda Red Force” on any Aggression Force base. Named bases like Data Vault keep their names.

Decks are read from the decklist name (“Leader, Subtitle - Base”). Players without a submitted decklist aren’t counted in the breakdown. Share is each deck’s percentage of players with a decklist. If a file holds several rounds, the latest round is used. Aspects and card art come from swu-db.com; card art © Lucasfilm Ltd. and Fantasy Flight Games. Your files are processed in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.
